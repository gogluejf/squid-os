Skills

Teach your computer once. Reuse it forever.

Reusable workflows instead of longer prompts.

Every developer repeats the same workflows.

Planning a feature. Reviewing a pull request. Investigating production issues. Preparing a release.

Instead of solving these with longer prompts, Squid OS solves them with Skills.

Skills package knowledge, workflows, and deterministic logic into reusable building blocks that every project can share. They're versioned with Git, easy to evolve, and designed to reduce repetitive prompting.

The more you invest in Skills, the less you repeat yourself.

Ready from day one

Squid OS includes a carefully selected set of built-in Skills so you can be productive immediately.

Planning, execution, analytics, and Skill Builder are included out of the box. They share a consistent structure that's easy for both humans and AI models to understand, making your workflows predictable, reusable, and easy to improve over time.

Rather than starting from a blank prompt, you start with proven workflows that evolve alongside your projects.

Build on an open ecosystem

Your Skills shouldn't be locked into a single product.

Squid OS is compatible with the Agent Skills standard, allowing you to use community Skills alongside your own. Whether you build them yourself, share them with your team, or publish them for others, Skills remain portable, versioned with Git, and reusable across every project.

Because your knowledge is more valuable than any single AI model.

One conversational interface for your entire operating system. Built in Go, optimized for local AI.
