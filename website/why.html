Why Squid OS exists

Coding is only a portion of your workflow.

AI coding agents transformed how we write software, but software development doesn't begin and end in a repository.

Every day you move between projects, terminals, documentation, notes, configuration files, browsers, Git repositories, and countless operating system tasks. Your AI should understand that entire workspace — not just the folder you opened.

Squid OS was built around a simple idea: your operating system is the workspace, and conversation is the interface.

Instead of hiding behind autonomous loops, Squid OS keeps you in control. Every action is visible. Every tool call is inspectable. Every decision remains yours.

Because the best AI assistant isn't one that replaces you — it's one that helps you move faster without taking control away.

One conversational interface for your entire operating system. Built in Go, optimized for local AI.
