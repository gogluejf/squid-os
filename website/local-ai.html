Local AI

Built for the models you own

First-class local inference, from day one.

Local models have become incredibly capable, but most AI tools still treat them as second-class citizens.

Squid OS was designed with local inference from the beginning. Fast streaming, token efficiency, transparent execution, and long-running conversations all work naturally whether your models run on your own hardware or in the cloud.

Choose the models that fit your workflow. Squid OS provides the same experience regardless of where they're running.
